You can use the [GridlinesVisible](https://docs.devexpress.com/Blazor/DevExpress.Blazor.DxTreeList.GridlinesVisible) property to hide/display gridlines within the DevExpress Blazor [TreeList](https://docs.devexpress.com/Blazor/404942/treelist).

To evaluate the capabilities of this feature, use checkboxes above the TreeList component to change gridline and border visibility.
