You can use the [GridlinesVisible](https://docs.devexpress.com/Blazor/DevExpress.Blazor.DxGrid.GridlinesVisible?v=26.1) property to hide/display gridlines within the DevExpress Blazor [Grid](https://docs.devexpress.com/Blazor/403143/grid).

To evaluate the capabilities of this feature, use checkboxes above the Grid component to change gridline and border visibility.
